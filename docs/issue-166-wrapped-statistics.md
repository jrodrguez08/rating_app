# Wrapped candidate statistics

`calculateWrappedStatistics` computes one candidate for one registered tracked Team and exact approved tournament key. The Team, internal Competition and Season, provider Competition and season, edition and reviewed fixture membership follow the [Phase 2A contract](issue-164-wrapped-coverage.md). The same provider fixture can contribute independently to each club through its own Team-specific Match and result. A candidate carries `publicationStatus: candidate_only`.

## Evidence

- Supply the reviewed ledger, registered scope evidence, current Match records and eligible current published summaries. The calculator reruns Phase 2A coverage and rejects incomplete or unresolved evidence. For each consumed summary it records the Match ID, generation timestamp and reopen count. The trusted preparer must read the current summary and its Match revision consistently and supply that revision. The pure calculator verifies internal consistency and cannot authenticate the caller's records.
- Supply reviewed canonical identity records and direct provider aliases. Unknown rated Players, conflicting aliases and duplicate canonical rows within one Match fail closed. Canonical display names come from the reviewed identities. The candidate omits raw operator and reconciliation evidence.
- Optional participant and head-coach evidence must be bound to the Match and tracked Team. A confirmed entrant requires explicit authoritative participation and substitute status. The XI uses normalized position families on provider-sourced confirmed fixture participants. Operator-verified participant rows can establish an entrance, but their position may have fallen back to the Player catalog. A future source may qualify such positions if it can separately prove match-time provenance.
- For each positive-vote rating, recover the integer sum from the stored average multiplied by its vote count. Accept only safe integer sums within a floating-point tolerance of eight machine epsilons scaled by the product, with tolerance below half an integer. Reject values outside that precision contract. Reduce sums over vote counts to fractions and compare by integer cross multiplication. The candidate serializes numerator and denominator as decimal strings. Future display formatting may round the result.

## Provisional policy `wrapped-pilot-v1`

The overview counts rated Matches, valid ballot submissions, distinct canonical rated Players and the median ballots per rated Match. It carries both Phase 2A coverage denominators. A finalized zero-vote result contributes to coverage but has no rating. Ballot submissions count participation across Matches and cannot identify unique supporters.

Awards require at least three rated Matches and 30 valid ballots overall. Comparison Matches require at least ten ballots each. With `Q` qualifying Matches, the overall appearance floor is `max(3, ceil(Q / 2))`. Thresholds are explicit positive integers in the versioned policy and are echoed on the candidate for reproducibility.

| Statistic                | Formula and tie rules                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Player of the Tournament | Arithmetic mean of qualifying Match ratings, with one equal weight per appearance. Require the overall appearance floor. At equal exact means, prefer more qualifying appearances, then retain co-winners ordered by canonical ID.                                                                                                                                                      |
| Best individual Match    | Highest exact Player rating across qualifying Matches. Prefer more ballots at equal scores, then retain co-winners ordered by kickoff, Match ID and Player ID.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Highest-rated Match      | Mean of the canonical Player ratings within a qualifying Match. Prefer more ballots at equal scores, then retain co-winners ordered by kickoff and Match ID.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Supporter-rating trend   | Compare the means of the Match scores in the earliest and latest `floor(Q / 2)` qualifying Matches. Skip the middle Match when odd and require at least three Matches per period. This measures supporter ratings.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Best XI                  | Fill a 4-3-3 from Players meeting the overall floor. Require at least three appearances in the position family and a strict majority of that Player's qualifying appearances in the family, counting unknown positions in the denominator. Rank by equal-Match mean for the family, then family appearances. At an exact cutoff tie, pick by canonical ID and retain tied alternatives. |
| Best substitute          | Mean over at least three confirmed entrances in qualifying Matches. Starts contribute zero weight. Use the appearance-award tie rules. Omit if qualifying rated appearances lack trustworthy roles.                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Coach of the Tournament  | Mean over qualifying fixture-specific head-coach assignments that agree with the published coach rating after alias resolution. Apply the overall appearance floor and appearance-award tie rules. Omit if a qualifying Match lacks reliable coach evidence. Coach ratings are separate from Player and Match scores.                                                                   |

Missing sample, evidence or formation yields a stable omission code. Award labels and omission copy belong in the shared Spanish and English i18n layer when presentation is added.

## Bounds and delivery

The calculator performs zero database or provider reads. The ledger caps fixtures at 512 and each summary at 30 rated Player rows. For `F` fixtures, `P` rated rows and `E` supplied identity, alias and participation records, evidence indexing and grouping take linear space and approximately linear time before sorting. Ranking adds `O(F log F + P log P)` comparisons, with integer arithmetic cost dependent on fraction size. A future offline preparer should reuse the ledger's exact Match and current summary reads and batch deduplicated identity, alias and fixture evidence reads for those Matches. The volume is proportional to the supplied edition and its evidence. Private ballots are outside that read plan.

Owner decisions still include approval of policy thresholds and actual tournament coverage, plus editorial review of omissions. A later snapshot workflow must define sanitized persistence, revision invalidation, explicit approval and publication. Phase 2B supplies the candidate calculation only.
